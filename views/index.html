How it works

Three simple steps

Recording attendance takes less than a minute.

1

Log in

Sign in with the account your administrator created for you. You will be asked to choose your own password the first time.

2

Check in at the office

Allow location access when your browser asks. The system confirms you are at the approved office, then records your arrival.

3

Check out when you leave

Check out at the end of your day. Your status and history are always available on your dashboard.

Features

Built for staff and administrators

Each person sees only what they need.

For staff

A personal dashboard with everything about your own attendance.

  • Today's check-in and check-out times
  • Clear status: on time, late, or incomplete
  • Your full attendance history
  • Request a review if your device or location fails
  • Change your own password

For administrators

Live oversight and the tools to manage attendance fairly.

  • Dashboard of check-ins, late arrivals and absences
  • Manage staff accounts and passwords
  • Configure working days, hours and grace period
  • Set the office location and allowed radius
  • Export reports as CSV or PDF
  • Corrections always require a recorded reason

Secure and private by design

Attendance times are set by the server, passwords are stored securely, and staff can only see their own records. Location is used only to confirm you are at the office, and no location history is kept.
